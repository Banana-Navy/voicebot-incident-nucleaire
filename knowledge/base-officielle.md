# Controlled knowledge base — permanent guidance

Status: permanent reference material for human review. This document is not a real-time operational source and must never be used to confirm that an incident is underway.

## Belgian protective priorities

1. **Immediate danger:** call 112 for injuries, fire, explosion, urgent medical need, suspected exposure or another immediate threat.
2. **Shelter first:** enter the nearest suitable building and remain indoors while awaiting official instructions.
3. **Limit outside air:** close doors and windows and switch off ventilation, air conditioning and other systems that draw in outside air, if this can be done safely.
4. **Move inside:** use an internal room where practical and keep pets indoors.
5. **Follow official information:** monitor BE-Alert, radio, television, the National Crisis Center and the competent authorities.
6. **Do not self-evacuate:** evacuation is an exceptional measure decided and organised by the authorities.
7. **Do not collect children from school during an alert:** schools apply their emergency procedures, and unnecessary travel may increase exposure and obstruct emergency operations.
8. **Do not take iodine tablets without an explicit authority instruction:** stable iodine protects only the thyroid against radioactive iodine. It does not protect the rest of the body or replace sheltering.

EURDEP readings, news coverage, social-media posts, historical records and caller observations are not authority instructions and must not be presented as such.

## Stable iodine

- Stable iodine saturates the thyroid and reduces uptake of radioactive iodine.
- It does not protect against other radioactive materials or external radiation.
- It is effective only when taken at the correct time and on explicit instruction from the competent authorities.
- It never replaces sheltering, evacuation when ordered or medical care.
- Dosage, contraindication and individual medical questions belong to the official leaflet, a pharmacist or a doctor.

## INES

The International Nuclear and Radiological Event Scale is a communication scale from Level 0 to Level 7. It expresses the safety significance of a nuclear or radiological event. The scale does not compare the general safety of facilities and does not itself determine protective measures.
